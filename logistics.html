Trade Terms

Incoterms & Pricing Structures

FOB Mombasa

Free On Board — Goods delivered to Mombasa port, loaded on vessel. Buyer arranges and pays ocean freight and insurance from that point. Lowest landed-cost option for buyers with established freight relationships.

Best for: Experienced importers

CIF US Port

Cost, Insurance & Freight — We arrange and pay ocean freight and insurance to your nominated US port. Buyer takes responsibility at port of entry. Simplest option for buyers new to direct import.

Best for: First-time importers

DAP Customer Dock

Delivered At Place — Full door-to-door service. We coordinate ocean freight, US customs clearance, drayage, and delivery to your facility in the Rocky Mountain region. Buyer handles import duties only.

Best for: Turnkey buyers
Forward Contracts Available: For buyers who require supply certainty, we offer multi-shipment forward purchase agreements. Contact us via the RFQ form to discuss pricing and terms.
